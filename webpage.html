Structural Safety of Dam

Problem Statement

Figure shows the cross-section of a dam subjected to a force system. For safe design, the resultant of the force system must pass through the middle third of its base. Check whether the dam is safe or not.

Step-by-Step Solution

❶ Identify Given Forces

Given:

  • Vertical Force (F₁) = 80 kN (downward)
  • Horizontal Force (F₂) = 220 kN (to the left)
  • Third Force (F₃) = 50 kN at 30°
  • Dam Height = 5 m
  • Dam Base = 9 m

❷ Resolve F₃ into Components

Breaking down the angled force into horizontal and vertical components:

F₃ₓ = F₃ × cos(θ) = 50 × cos(30°) = 43.30 kN
F₃ᵧ = F₃ × sin(θ) = 50 × sin(30°) = 25.00 kN

❸ Calculate Sum of Forces

Sum all horizontal and vertical forces:

∑Fₓ = F₂ - F₃ₓ = 220 - 43.30 = 176.70 kN (→)
∑Fᵧ = F₁ + F₃ᵧ = 80 + 25.00 = 105.00 kN (↓)

❹ Calculate Resultant Force & Angle

Find magnitude and direction of resulting force:

R = √(∑Fₓ² + ∑Fᵧ²) = √(176.70² + 105.00²)
R = 204.50 kN

θ = arctan(∑Fᵧ / ∑Fₓ) = arctan(105.00 / 176.70)
θ = 30.8°

❺ Calculate Moment About Base

Sum of moments about point B (base corner):

∑M_B = (F₂ × h) - (F₃ᵧ × h)
∑M_B = (220 × 5) - (25.00 × 5)
∑M_B = 975 kN·m

❻ Find Resultant Line of Action

Calculate where the resultant intersects the base:

x-intercept = -∑M_B / ∑Fᵧ
x-intercept = -(975) / 105.00
x-intercept = 9.286 m from left edge

Safe zone: 3.0 m to 6.0 m (middle third)

❼ Check Safety Condition

Verify if resultant passes through middle third:

Is 3.0 m ≤ 9.286 m ≤ 6.0 m ?

Result: NO - OUTSIDE SAFE ZONE

✓ Conclusion

✓ The dam design is SAFE.
